System design

Unwall

Turn a video you own into a source page that AI answer engines can read, cite, and link back to. Everything runs on your machine except two model calls and the static pages you approve.

What leaves the machine

  • Audio to OpenAI for transcription.
  • Transcript and frames to Anthropic for the draft.
  • Approved pages to Railway as a static bundle.
  • The page URL to IndexNow, with a key the host proves it owns.
  • Nothing else. Keys, videos, and job data stay local.

Job states

Processing queued→ reading→ listening→ watching→ writing→ review→ published Going live running→ liveor failed
  • Editing a published page moves it back to review. The live copy stays as approved until you publish again.

When something fails

  • Deploy rejected. The page is not recorded as live, and Try again reruns the deploy.
  • Files don't match in 4 minutes. The launch fails instead of guessing.
  • IndexNow refuses the key. The page stays live and the submission shows as failed, with a retry.
  • App restarts mid-job. Interrupted jobs are marked failed on startup, so nothing spins forever.
